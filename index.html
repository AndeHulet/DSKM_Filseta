Spiritual Significance

What is Tsome Filseta (ጾመ ፍልሰታ)?

Filseta (ፍልሰታ) signifies the separation and bodily ascension of the Blessed Virgin Mary to Heaven. Following the tradition of the Holy Apostles who fasted for two weeks to behold her resurrection and ascension, the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church observes this fast each year with deep devotion.

Faithful believers—young and old—gather daily in the church compound for Subae (ሱባኤ), partaking in Holy Communion, prostrations, and spiritual teachings.

Traditional Ethiopian Iconography of St. Mary (ቅድስት ድንግል ማርያም)
